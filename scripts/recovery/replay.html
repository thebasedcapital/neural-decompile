Evaluation contract

Protocol and provenance

This is an offline replay of public, deidentified human neural recordings for imagined 7DoF motion. Evaluation uses a reserved local second recording block, not official minival or the private test. It is not live BCI control, physical robot feedback, a clinical safety result, or an official leaderboard score. R² is a regression metric, not accuracy; values may be negative.
